# PH Bulwark — ready for testing 🛡️

**A free, non-profit child-safety shield from Predator Hunters.**
Four years in the making. Now opening for testers.

---

## What it is

**Predator Hunters Bulwark** (PH Bulwark) is a free, open-source system that helps
guardians keep children safer online — built by the Predator Hunters community, **not
for profit**, after **4 years of prototyping**.

It runs **on the child's own device, with the child's and guardian's knowledge** —
a transparent safety tool, not hidden spyware. It:

- **Blocks adult and unsafe content in real time** — web, images, and video — using
  on-device analysis, not crude whole-site blocklists.
- **Spots signs of grooming** in messages on the device, even in apps that encrypt
  in transit.
- **Tells a guardian** the moment it steps in — by email and phone push — with the
  context they need to decide, and a one-tap **Approve / Keep-blocked**.
- **Resists tampering** — it raises an alert if protection is switched off or removed.
- **Respects privacy by design.** Analysis happens on the device; only redacted
  summaries leave it. **Suspected illegal child-sexual-abuse material is blocked and
  reported-by-hash only — never viewed, stored, or sent anywhere.**

## Why it's different

- **Free & non-profit.** No subscriptions, no data sold, ever. Built by volunteers.
- **Open source.** Anyone can read exactly what it does — and what it doesn't.
- **Your server, or ours.** Use **PH Bulwark Cloud**, or **self-host** the whole
  thing on your own machine (Bitwarden-style) — your family's data, your choice.
- **Honest about limits.** We tell you what it can and can't catch. No magic claims.

## Status: testing 🧪

After four years of prototyping, the engineering is built and continuously tested.
**We're now opening it up for real-world testing** — we're looking for:

- **Guardians** willing to trial it on a managed family device and tell us what works.
- **Self-hosters** comfortable running the server and giving feedback.
- **Contributors** — Rust, Kotlin, security, and safeguarding expertise especially.

> This is a **testing release**, not a finished product. Expect rough edges, help us
> file them, and never rely on any single tool as a child's only protection.
